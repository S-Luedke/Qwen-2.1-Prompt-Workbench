Worth knowing

    CFG stays at 1 on the official path, which means the negative prompt is unused. The ComfyUI templates ship 25 steps with euler / simple; Qwen's own repository uses about 40 to 50. Higher-order solvers converge in fewer steps, so res_2s with beta57 lands in the same compute band at roughly 20 and is what these templates were checked against. Keep the canvas at 1 megapixel or above: below that, flat areas hold visible noise.
